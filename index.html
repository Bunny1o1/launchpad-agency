FAQ

Common questions

What stage of startup is this best for? +
Saasible works best with B2B SaaS founders who have a working product but no GTM motion yet — typically pre-revenue or under ₹10L MRR. If you've had some conversations but nothing is converting consistently, you're in the right place.
Why WhatsApp and Voice AI specifically? +
In India, your buyers are on WhatsApp. Cold email open rates are under 20%. WhatsApp open rates are above 80%. Voice AI lets you run outbound calls at scale without a sales team. We build where attention actually is — not where the templates say it should be.
How is this different from hiring a sales person? +
A sales hire costs ₹8–15 LPA and takes 3 months to ramp. They bring hustle — not systems. We bring the outbound infrastructure, the sequences, and the playbooks on day one. You can hire a sales person to run the system we build. That's the right order of operations.
Do I need to know anything about WhatsApp APIs or VAPI? +
No. We handle the technical setup — API connections, automation flows, voice agent configuration, integrations. You focus on the product. We build the GTM engine.
What does the GTM Build actually deliver at the end of 30 days? +
A fully built outbound system: WhatsApp automation flows, a Voice AI agent (if applicable), 30 days of campaign data, a Notion handoff doc, and Loom walkthroughs of every component. You own everything. We hand off the keys.
Can you guarantee results? +
No. Anyone who guarantees pipeline numbers in GTM is lying to you. What we guarantee: the system gets built, the campaigns get launched, and you get honest weekly reporting on what's working and what needs to change. Results depend on your product, your market, and your ICP clarity — which is exactly what the diagnostic is designed to sharpen.
